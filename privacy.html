Privacy Policy

Gadgetka Journeys
Last updated: October 14, 2026

Gadgetka Journeys is a personal video-processing and social-media publishing application operated by Tomáš Roháček. This policy explains how the application handles information when connecting to TikTok.

Information accessed

When the operator authorizes TikTok access, the application may receive authorization credentials (such as access and refresh tokens) and account information made available by the permissions granted. The application uses these credentials to upload videos to TikTok for the authorized account. The exact information accessible depends on the permissions approved by TikTok and the account owner.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized account, upload selected videos, and support the publishing workflow. Videos are prepared and processed locally on the operator's computer before upload. Uploads may be completed manually within TikTok, depending on the publishing method used.

Storage and sharing

Authorization credentials and local processing files are intended to be stored on the operator's own computer and are not published on this website. Video content and necessary upload information are transmitted to TikTok when an upload is initiated. TikTok processes information under its own privacy policy. The operator does not sell TikTok account information or use it for advertising profiling.

Retention and security

Local credentials are kept only while needed for the authorized integration and may be deleted when access is no longer required. Reasonable precautions should be used to restrict access to locally stored credentials. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Revoking access and deletion

The TikTok account owner can revoke the application's access through TikTok's connected-app settings. To request deletion of any locally held TikTok authorization data, contact the operator at the email below. Revoking access does not automatically remove content already posted on TikTok; such content can be managed through TikTok.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. The current version will be posted on this page.
